💬 Key Phrases
Learn important phrases about jobs. Click 🔊 to hear them spoken.
🔵 Say what job you have
I am a doctor.
Use "I am a ___" to say your job.
She is a teacher. / He is a teacher.
Use "She is / He is" for someone else.
I am not a doctor. I am a nurse.
Use this to correct a mistake.
🟢 Say where you work
I work at a hospital.
Use at + a specific building.
She works at a school.
Add s to "work" for he/she/it.
He works in an office.
Use in for a room or indoor space.
They work on a farm.
Use on for land or transport.
🟠 Questions about jobs
"What do you do?" → "I am a chef."
Ask someone about their job.
"Where do you work?" → "I work at a restaurant."
Ask where someone works.
"Do you like your job?" → "Yes, I love my job!"
Ask if someone enjoys their work.
"What time do you start?" → "I start at nine o'clock."
Ask about work hours.
🟣 Job interview phrases
"Tell me about yourself." → "My name is Ana. I am from Poland."
Introduce yourself in an interview.
"I can work hard." / "I am a good worker."
Talk about your skills and qualities.
"I worked at a hotel for two years."
Talk about past experience — use worked (past).
"Can you start on Monday?" → "Yes, I can start on Monday."
Confirm when you can begin work.
📖 Grammar note:
I am a doctor.  ·  He/She is a doctor.  ·  They are doctors.
I work at a hospital.  ·  He/She works at a hospital.

💼 1 — Meeting someone new
👩
Maria
Hello! My name is Maria. What is your name?
👨
James
Hi Maria! I am James. Nice to meet you.
👩
Maria
Nice to meet you too! What do you do, James?
👨
James
I am a bus driver. I work at the bus station. What about you?
👩
Maria
I am a nurse. I work at the hospital.
👨
James
That is a great job! Do you like it?
👩
Maria
Yes, I love my job. I help sick people every day.
🔑 Key phrases: "What do you do?" · "I am a ___." · "I work at ___." · "Do you like it?"
📋 2 — Job interview
👔
Manager
Good morning. Please sit down. Can you tell me about yourself?
👩
Ana
Good morning. My name is Ana. I am from Romania. I am 32 years old.
👔
Manager
What work experience do you have?
👩
Ana
I worked as a cleaner in a hotel for three years. I am a hard worker.
👔
Manager
Can you work weekends?
👩
Ana
Yes, I can work weekends and evenings.
👔
Manager
Very good. Thank you Ana. We will call you soon.
👩
Ana
Thank you very much. Goodbye!
🔑 Key phrases: "I worked as a ___." · "I am a hard worker." · "I can work ___." · "Thank you very much."
🏪 3 — At the supermarket
🛒
Customer
Excuse me. Where is the bread, please?
🏪
Shop assistant
Go straight. Turn right. The bread is on the left.
🛒
Customer
Thank you! Do you work here every day?
🏪
Shop assistant
Yes, I work Monday to Friday. I am a shop assistant.
🛒
Customer
Do you like your job?
🏪
Shop assistant
Yes, I like helping people. It is a good job!
🔑 Key phrases: "Excuse me." · "Go straight." · "Turn right/left." · "I work Monday to Friday."
🏥 4 — At the doctor
😷
Patient (Carlos)
Hello. I have an appointment. My name is Carlos.
👩‍⚕️
Receptionist
Hello Carlos. Please sit and wait. The doctor will see you soon.
👨‍⚕️
Doctor
Hello Carlos. I am Doctor Smith. What is the problem?
😷
Carlos
I have a headache and I feel very tired.
👨‍⚕️
Doctor
I see. Do you work long hours?
😷
Carlos
Yes. I am a delivery driver. I work ten hours a day.
👨‍⚕️
Doctor
You need to rest. Here is your prescription. Take these tablets twice a day.
🔑 Vocabulary: appointment · prescription · tablets · receptionist · headache
🍽️ 5 — At the restaurant
🍽️
Waiter
Good evening! Welcome. How many people?
👫
Customer
Two people, please.
🍽️
Waiter
This way, please. Here is the menu. Are you ready to order?
👫
Customer
Yes. I would like the chicken, please.
🍽️
Waiter
Of course. And to drink?
👫
Customer
Water, please. Excuse me — do you like working here?
🍽️
Waiter
Yes! I am a waiter. I work here five days a week. I like meeting people.
🔑 Key phrases: "I would like ___." · "Are you ready to order?" · "I work ___ days a week." · "This way, please."

📝 Writing Practice
Read each task and write your answer. Use the examples to help you.
✍️ How to practise: Write your answer. Read it aloud — this helps speaking too! You can write on screen or on paper. There is no wrong answer — just try your best!
1. Write your name and your job (or a job you want).
My name is and I am a .
Example: My name is Maria and I am a nurse.
2. Write where you work or want to work.
I work at .
Example: I work at a school. / I work in an office.
3. Write 2 sentences about a doctor.
Example: A doctor helps sick people. A doctor works at a hospital.
4. Write about your day at work. Use: start, finish, do.
Example: I start work at 8 o'clock. I teach children. I finish at 4 o'clock.
5. Write 3 sentences about a job you know.
Example: He is a mechanic. He works at a garage. He fixes cars every day.
6. Fill in the blanks. Choose from: is · are · work · works · at · in
She a teacher.
He at a hospital.
They farmers.
I at a supermarket.
She works an office.
He a chef.

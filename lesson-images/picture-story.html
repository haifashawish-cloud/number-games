THE LOST WALLET

A Picture Story  |  Past Simple  |  Pre-Intermediate

Look at the pictures and read the story.

Picture 1
Scene 1 It was a sunny Saturday afternoon. Sarah was walking down Queen Street after shopping.
Picture 2
Scene 2 Suddenly, she saw something on the footpath. It was a black wallet!
Picture 3
Scene 3 She picked it up. Inside, she found money, cards, and a driver's licence.
Picture 4
Scene 4 The licence had a name and an address: Mr Patel, 24 Valley Road, Mt Eden.
Picture 5
Scene 5 Sarah took the bus to Mt Eden. She found the house and knocked on the door.
Picture 6
Scene 6 An elderly man opened the door. He looked very worried and sad.
Picture 7
Scene 7 Sarah gave him the wallet. Mr Patel smiled and said, "Thank you, thank you!"
Picture 8
Scene 8 Mr Patel made tea and cake. They sat together and talked for an hour.
